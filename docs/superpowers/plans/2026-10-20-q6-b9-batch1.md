## Problem

Shopflix (and some Snif and e-shop) titles carry Greek descriptors and Greek look-alike capitals that the
case/fan/cooler normalizers keep in the model name. `names.model_key` keeps only a-z0-9, so the Greek words vanish
from the key by themselves; what splits a model is the Latin left next to them and the look-alike letters:

- Shopflix AIO "Asus ROG Ryujin III 240 Διπλού Ανεμιστήρα 120mm για Socket ..." becomes key
  `asusrogryujiniii240120mm`, not `asusrogryujiniii240` like Skroutz and BestPrice. Shopflix also cuts the word
  "Υδρόψυξη" off the front of its AIO titles, so 259 Shopflix AIOs (and 9 Snif ones) were typed **Air** with no
  radiator.
- e-shop "DARKFLASH ΚΟΥΤΙ ΥΠΟΛΟΓΙΣΤΗ C285", "... ΠΕΡΙΠΤΩΣΗ ΥΠΟΛΟΓΙΣΤΗ ΧΩΡΙΣ ΑΝΕΜΙΣΤΗΡΕΣ" keep the Greek in the name.
- Greek capitals typed for Latin: BestPrice "Antec ΑΧ61 Elite" (key `antec61elite`), "Lian Li Uni Fan Ιnfinity SL",
  "NZXT F120 RGB Duο", Snif "Lian Li Hydroshift Ιι Lcd 360" (the Hydroshift II, keyed as the first Hydroshift).
- Maker names written two ways: "Thermaltake Tower 300" (one BestPrice listing) vs "The Tower 300"; Lian Li
  "O11D EVO RGB" (Skroutz, Shopflix, e-shop) vs "O11 Dynamic Evo RGB" (BestPrice, Snif), and RGB dropped from both,
  so the EVO RGB (455.7 mm GPU) shared keys with the plain EVO (426 mm) and needed an `overrides.json` entry.

## Change (rules, files)

1. `scraper/names.py`: `latin()` (moved from `normalize_mobo._latin`, shared) turns Greek look-alike capitals into
   Latin in tokens that have a Latin letter or digit ("ΑΧ61" -> AX61, "120ΜΜ" -> 120MM), and in words that mix
   Latin and Greek letters when nothing Greek is left ("Ιnfinity", "Duο"); "Ιι"/"ΙΙ" alone = II. Greek words
   ("ΚΟΥΤΙ", "ΜΑΥΡΟ", "ΜΕ") stay Greek. `prepare()` = whitespace + `latin()` + split a Latin word glued to a Greek one
   ("Towerμε" -> "Tower με"). Boards call `latin(mixed=False)` (capitals pass only, exactly as before: 0 of 2,939
   board listings change).
2. `scraper/normalize_case.py`: parses `names.prepare(title)` (the stored title stays as the site wrote it); cuts
   at "Περίπτωση/ΠΕΡΙΠΤΩΣΗ", "Χωρίς/ΧΩΡΙΣ", "για", "Columnless"; drops a leading "ΚΟΥΤΙ ΥΠΟΛΟΓΙΣΤΗ"; translates
   "ΓΥΑΛΙΝΗ/Γυάλινη" -> Glass and "ΑΚΡΥΛΙΚΗ/Ακρυλική" -> Acrylic (Darkflash panel versions that other sites write in
   English); `maker_name()`: Thermaltake "Tower <n>" -> "The Tower <n>", Lian Li "O11D" -> "O11 Dynamic", and "RGB"
   kept after "O11 Dynamic EVO" when the title says "EVO RGB" (NOISE still drops RGB everywhere else).
3. `scraper/normalize_cooling.py`, fans: parse `names.prepare(title)`; drop Shopflix's leading "Kit LED ανεμιστήρων";
   "Σετ" = Set (noise); translate "Ασύρματο" -> Wireless and "LCD Οθόνη" -> LCD (Lian Li's wireless fans are their own
   products; word order as BestPrice writes it: "SL Wireless LCD Reverse"); e-shop's Greek "120ΜΜ" in mid-name only
   loses the unit (making it Latin would cut the name there and merge the Swafan EX12 Sync edition into the plain one).
4. `scraper/normalize_cooling.py`, coolers: parse `names.prepare(title)`; cut from "Μονού/Διπλού/Τριπλού/Τετραπλού
   Ανεμιστήρα", "Επεξεργαστή", "Παθητική", "Ενεργητική" (never the first word); e-shop "ΣΥΣΤΗΜΑ ΥΔΡΟΨΥΞΗΣ" in front
   dropped. Water marks: the title's water words as before, **or** the URL ("udropsuxe/ydropsuxi/ydropsyksi" -
   Shopflix and Snif slugs), **or** Shopflix's "<count> Ανεμιστήρα 120/140mm". Radiator, in order: Skroutz spec
   "(3x120mm)"; a 240/280/360/420/480 in the name or URL; a bare 120/140 only with a water word or water URL
   ("Mars Gaming ML-ONE120"); else count x fan size from the Greek words (2 x 120 = 240). **The "120mm" after
   "Τριπλού Ανεμιστήρα" is never taken as the radiator.** An AIO name that still ends in its fan size (Snif "Corsair
   Nautilus 360 RS 120mm - Μαύρο") loses it, then the radiator is added when the name lacks it (as today). Only a
   water URL with nothing to size it stays Air without a radiator, as before (that URL category also lists air
   towers, "MSI MAG CoreFrozr AA13 120mm").
5. `scraper/model_aliases.json`: **242 new entries** (case 10, fan 7, cooler 225), generated by the rule Q3 used
   (every listing of the old key moves to one new key, the old key has no listings left), plus one picked by hand
   (case `lianlio11devo` -> `lianlio11dynamicevorgb`, owner question 1). File ready at
   `work/model_aliases_q6.json` (main's ram entries + the three new categories).
6. `scraper/overrides.json`: the O11 Dynamic EVO RGB entry now names the new key `lianlio11dynamicevorgb` (no title
   regex needed). It is **kept, not deleted**: see "O11 Dynamic EVO RGB" below (owner question 2).
7. `scraper/makers/lianli.py` `norm()`: "O11D" reads as "O11DYNAMIC", so the maker page `/product/o11d-evo-rgb/`
   ranks for the renamed listings and, once fetched, beats the plain EVO page by the longest-prefix rule.
8. `scripts/checks/renormalize_diff.py`: runs fans/coolers (`make_fan_listing`/`make_cooler_listing`) and gives the
   old side REF's `names.py`. Caveat: latest.json stores cooler titles without " Υδρόψυξη CPU" and case titles
   without " Κουτί Υπολογιστή", so for coolers its numbers are inflated (1,010 "changes" on stored titles, most are
   artefacts); `work/numbers.py` rebuilds each listing's real input first (4,015 of 4,015 coolers rebuilt exactly)
   and is the source of the numbers below.

### Judgment calls (shown, not just stripped)

- **Greek words that name a real difference are translated**: Ασύρματο -> Wireless, LCD Οθόνη -> LCD (fans);
  Γυάλινη/ΓΥΑΛΙΝΗ -> Glass, Ακρυλική -> Acrylic (cases: Darkflash DK431 Glass and Aquarius Acrylic are versions).
  Λευκό/Λευκή/Μαύρο are colours, and `names.COLORS` removes colours in all three categories, so they are dropped.
- **Dual vs single air towers**: Shopflix writes "Διπλού Ανεμιστήρα" on air towers **without** a fan size, and the
  Greek words never reached the key, so no air-tower key changes in this batch (only the shown name loses the Greek).
  No merged group mixes Διπλού/Dual and Μονού/Single titles (0). Three air towers already group a Shopflix
  "Διπλού" listing next to a separate "<name> Dual" model; unchanged by Q6, listed below (owner question 5).
- **AIO radiator kept**: every Shopflix AIO gets its radiator from the name (240/360...) or count x size; e.g.
  "Be Quiet Light Loop Διπλού Ανεμιστήρα 120mm" -> Light Loop **240**, "... Τριπλού Ανεμιστήρα 120mm" -> Light Loop
  **360** (the old key `bequietlightloop120mm` held both; it splits, so it gets no alias). Shopflix's own errors
  are overruled by the name: "Thermaltake TH420 V2 Ultra Τριπλού Ανεμιστήρα 120mm" -> TH420 (420), "Endorfy Navis
  F360 Διπλού Ανεμιστήρα 120mm" -> F360 (360).
- **The Tower 300**: the maker's name; BestPrice "Thermaltake Tower 300 Gravel Sand" joins the 22 "The Tower 300"
  listings (the Thermaltake rule only touches a name that starts "Tower <digits>").
- **O11 Dynamic EVO RGB**: one model for all five sites (8 listings), the plain EVO stays its own model.

## O11 Dynamic EVO RGB and its override

Keeping RGB in the name is not enough to retire the override: `makers/__init__.apply` matches a listing to the
longest maker part number that **starts** its name, and the cached plain EVO page (`o11-dynamic-evo`, part
`O11DYNAMICEVO`, 426 mm) is a prefix of `O11DYNAMICEVORGB`. The `o11d-evo-rgb` page has never been fetched (not in
`public/data/makers/lianli.json`). Offline proof: with the override aimed at a key nobody has, `finish` gives every
EVO RGB listing 426 mm (shop 455.7, maker 426, SAFER takes the smaller), which would mark GPUs 427-455 mm "no" and
hide them for this case. So this batch (a) retargets the entry to `lianlio11dynamicevorgb` (offline: "override ...
on 8 listings"), (b) makes `lianli.norm` read "O11D" as "O11 Dynamic" so the next scrape ranks the `o11d-evo-rgb`
page (the EVO RGB listings lack `coolerMaxMm` from the shops, so they are "needy") and, once cached, it wins the
longest-prefix match. A follow-up commit deletes the entry when the cache shows that page with 455.7.

History: the hand alias `lianlio11devo -> lianlio11dynamicevorgb` moves the EVO RGB's 365 points (338 imported from
the Skroutz EVO RGB product + 27 own Skroutz/Shopflix) and its import marker to the new model. The old key
`lianlio11dynamicevo` keeps its 27 days of BestPrice EVO **RGB** prices (147.99) and now belongs to the plain EVO
(191.90): its chart starts with RGB prices and its week change reads +29.7 % on 25/10, until a week passes (owner
question 3).
