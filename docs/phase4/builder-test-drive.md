## Worker's view

1. **What looked wrong:** the PSU step (spec groups, no SFX, 1000 W hidden for the 5090, junk first: #1–4); Recommended that ignores use and budget (#5); budget hints that don't match prices (#6); wrong measurements giving both wrong "fits" and wrong "hidden" (#9–12); box coolers unknown (#7).
2. **Guided vs Quick list:** Guided is the better path (search, sort, chips, Review, share). Quick list contradicts it on optional parts (#8), has only cheapest-first pickers full of junk and no Review/Copy/share (#21), and its picker opens under the phone bar (#24). Annoyance in Guided: 11 steps with a long text block each before the products (#23, #29).
3. **Hidden parts that fit / "fits" that don't:** hidden: H9 Flow (#9), Assassin X 120 Refined SE on AM4 (#10), B550M-ITX/ac in ITX cases (#11), all 1000 W PSUs for c) (#3). Wrong "fits": AK400 in Era ITX, ATX board in SG13, ATX PSU in Terra (#2, #12). All come from one wrong field per product; a "fits" from a single source value is trusted as much as a measured one.
4. **Too many "Fit not verified"?** Yes: ~41% of cases and ~33% of coolers in the Quick list pickers, every case with an HDD, almost every case with a 360 AIO (#14). Rank them below verified parts by default (don't hide: hiding would also hide the right answer when data is missing), say the reason once per step, and use the fields that exist (`bays35`).
